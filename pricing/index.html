Pricing FAQ

Common questions

How does payment work?

Typically 50% upfront to begin and 50% on delivery. Larger projects are split into milestones.

Are there any hidden costs?

No. The quote lists everything. Third-party costs such as domains or hosting are shown separately.

What if I need changes after launch?

Free support covers bugs during the support window. New features are quoted separately or covered by a maintenance plan.
