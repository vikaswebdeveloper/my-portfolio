Frequently asked questions

How long does a project take?

A small website usually takes 1–2 weeks. Larger web apps or AI projects take 3–8 weeks depending on scope. You'll get a timeline in your quote.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of your goals, any branding (logo, colours), reference sites you like, and your content or a plan for creating it.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Once the final payment is made, you own the source code and all deliverables. See the Terms of Service.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes — I can redesign, fix, speed up or extend existing sites and apps.

Do you help with hosting and domains?

Yes. I set up hosting (such as Vercel or Render), connect your domain and enable HTTPS.

Can you add AI features to my product?

Yes — Q&A over your documents (RAG), prediction tools and other AI features, with clear limits and disclaimers where needed.

What is your revision policy?

Each milestone includes revision rounds so the result matches what you asked for. Extra scope is quoted separately.

What if I'm not satisfied?

See the Refund & Cancellation policy. I'll always try to put things right first.

Still have a question?

Send me a message and I'll get back within 24 hours.
