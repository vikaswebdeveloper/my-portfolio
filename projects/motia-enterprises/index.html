Overview

The brief

Motia Enterprises supplies radiator auto parts such as fan covers, plastic fibre tanks and coolant tanks. The site opens with a full-width product hero, then lists the range clearly, with About, Product and Contact pages. I built and launched it in React.js in March–April 2024, optimising performance and keeping the layout consistent across mobile and desktop.

What was delivered

  • Responsive, mobile-friendly layout
  • Optimised performance
  • Consistent layout on mobile and desktop
  • Product range listed clearly, with a contact page
  • Deployed to a live public URL
