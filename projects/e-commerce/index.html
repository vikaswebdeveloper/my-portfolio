Overview

The problem & the solution

Problem. An online store has to get the details right: correct prices, accurate stock, verified payments and safe handling of customer data. A pretty storefront isn't enough.

Solution. Shop is a complete store with a customer storefront and an admin panel. Prices are computed on the server, Razorpay payments are verified by signature, stock is protected against overselling, and an automated test suite checks the security-critical paths.

This is a demo store with sample products. It shows the complete flow, from browsing and checkout to order management.

Highlights

  • Customer storefront and admin panel in one platform
  • Razorpay checkout with server-verified payments, plus Cash on Delivery
  • Product images stored on Backblaze B2
  • Coupons, wishlist, reviews and order tracking
  • Audit log of every admin action
  • Security-focused automated tests (Vitest and Supertest)
What's inside

Built for shoppers and for store owners

🛍️

Customer storefront

  • Sign up, log in and reset passwords
  • Live search suggestions and filters by category, brand, price, rating and stock
  • Product pages with image gallery and verified-purchase reviews
  • Wishlist, cart with stock checks and saved addresses
  • Coupons and checkout with Razorpay or Cash on Delivery
  • Order history, tracking, cancel and refund
📊

Admin panel

  • Dashboard with revenue, orders, a 7-day chart and low-stock alerts
  • Product management with image upload
  • Category and coupon management
  • Order management with a validated status flow
  • Customer list with suspend and reactivate
  • Review moderation and a full audit log
🔐

Security & testing

  • bcrypt password hashing, strong-password policy and login lockout
  • Razorpay HMAC-SHA256 signature checks, with a webhook as a second confirmation
  • Prices and order totals computed on the server
  • Uploads verified by file signature, with size limits
  • Rate limiting, CSRF origin checks and security headers
  • Tests for auth, injection, XSS, payments and stock races
