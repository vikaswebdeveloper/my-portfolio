Cookie Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This page explains what this website stores in your browser.

1. Cookies set by this site

This site does not set tracking, advertising or analytics cookies.

2. Local storage

One item is saved in your browser's local storage: theme, which remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. It never leaves your device and is not used to identify you.

3. Third-party content

Project pages show screenshots and link to live websites hosted on services such as Vercel and Render. Once you open those sites they run independently and may set their own cookies, for example to keep you signed in. Their policies apply when you use them.

4. Your choices

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings, or block third-party cookies. Clearing the theme item simply resets the site to your system's light or dark setting.

5. Changes

If analytics or other cookies are added in future, this policy will be updated first. See also the Privacy Policy.
