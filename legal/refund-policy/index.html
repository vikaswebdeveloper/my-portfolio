Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. Cancelling a project

You may cancel at any time by writing to you@example.com. Work completed up to the cancellation date is billed.

2. Before work starts

If you cancel before any work has begun, the upfront payment is refunded in full, less any payment-gateway fees.

3. After work has started

The upfront payment covers work already done. Any unused portion of a milestone payment is refunded.

4. After delivery

Once a project is delivered and accepted, payments are non-refundable. Defects are fixed free of charge during the support period.

5. Dissatisfaction

If the delivered work materially differs from the agreed scope, we will first revise it. If that cannot be resolved, a partial refund may be agreed.

6. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

7. Maintenance plans

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days' notice. Fees already paid for the current month are not refunded.
