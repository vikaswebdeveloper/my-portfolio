Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details you choose to send, such as your name, email address and project description.
  • Preferences such as your light/dark theme choice, stored in your browser only.

This site has no accounts and does not run advertising trackers.

2. How the contact form works

When you submit the contact form, your name, email address, selected service, budget and message are sent through Web3Forms, a third-party form service, and delivered to my email inbox. This website does not store them on its own server. I use them only to reply to your enquiry. Web3Forms processes the submission under its own privacy policy.

3. How we use information

We use the details you send only to reply to your enquiry, prepare quotes and deliver the services you request.

4. Local storage

We store your theme preference in your browser's local storage. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

5. Third-party links and embeds

Project pages link to live websites hosted on services such as Vercel and Render. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only when required by law.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete any personal information we hold from our correspondence by emailing you@example.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

9. Contact
