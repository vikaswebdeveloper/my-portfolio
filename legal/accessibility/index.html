Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 21 November 2026

I want this site to be usable by everyone, including people using assistive technology.

What's in place

  • Semantic HTML with headings, landmarks and labelled form fields.
  • Light and dark themes, with a toggle that is keyboard accessible.
  • Responsive layout that works from phones to large screens.
  • Animations switch off automatically for visitors who set “reduce motion” in their system.
  • Descriptive text for images and clear link and button labels.

Known limitations

The live project websites linked from project pages are separate sites outside my control and may not meet the same standards. Project screenshots have descriptive alt text.

Feedback

If you find a barrier or need content in another format, email you@example.com. I aim to reply within 2 business days and fix issues promptly.
